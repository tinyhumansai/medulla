# Availability

Medulla is in early alpha and access is gated. It is rolling out to a small group of OpenHuman subscribers first. The group is small on purpose: alpha users get direct access to the team, and what they run is what decides what gets built next.

The client itself is open source under GPL-3.0-only at [`tinyhumansai/medulla-src`](https://github.com/tinyhumansai/medulla-src), and `medulla --mock` runs the whole terminal offline against a scripted runtime with no account. Signing in is what a live account adds: the hosted sign-in, usage meters, and the feedback board.

To request access, tell us which agents you run and on how many machines.
